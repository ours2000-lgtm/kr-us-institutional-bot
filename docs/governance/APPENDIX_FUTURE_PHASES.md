### Governance Notes

1. **No Binding Force**  
   This appendix has no binding force on the Cold-Execution Engine.  
   It does not change, weaken, or extend the README v1.0 conceptual contract unless explicitly promoted through a **Type A constitutional amendment**.

2. **Precedence**  
   In case of any discrepancy between this appendix and the Core Specification, the Core Specification shall always prevail.

3. **Versioning**  
   v0.x denotes non-binding draft iterations.  
   Minor increments (v0.1, v0.2) track exploratory drafts; major increments (v0.9 → v1.0) mark readiness for constitutional promotion.

4. **Usage**  
   Teams may reference this appendix for experimentation, research, and planning.  
   Content herein MUST NOT influence live verdicts, enforcement logic, or production behavior.

5. **Promotion Path**  
   To be promoted into the core specification, candidates must include:
   - a Proof of Concept (PoC),
   - a resource and risk impact analysis,
   - and constitutionally valid activation evidence.  
   All such evidence MUST be recorded as immutable entries in the Evidence Log.

6. **Cross-Reference Rule**  
   Appendix sections that extend or refine core concepts MUST explicitly reference the relevant section of the Core Specification.

7. **Lifecycle**  
   This roadmap is reviewed periodically and may be revised, deprecated, or replaced without affecting the binding contract.
