## 1. Purpose

본 모델은 Governance Capability 성숙도를 정량적으로 평가하고 조직의 Operational Resilience 수준을 측정하기 위한 공식 프레임이다.

## 3. Maturity Levels

### Level 1 — Ad hoc

Entry: Policy documentation rate ≥ 20%  
Exit: Policy documentation ≥ 80%  

---

### Level 2 — Defined

Entry: Policy documentation ≥ 80%  
Exit: Risk model operational + runbooks defined  

---

### Level 3 — Operational

Entry: Scenario testing operational  
Exit: Annual simulations ≥ 4  
Incident SLA compliance ≥ 90%  

Sub-bands:  

2.5–2.9 → Low Operational  
3.0–3.4 → High Operational  

---

### Level 4 — Measured ⭐

Entry: Governance metrics monitored  
Key domains score ≥ 3  

Exit: Continuous improvement operational  
Impact tolerance defined  

Sub-bands:

3.5–3.8 → Early Measured  
3.9–4.2 → Advanced Measured  

---

### Level 5 — Adaptive

Entry: Predictive governance  
At least one domain score ≥ 4  

Exit: Continuous resilience validation  

---

## 4. Capability Domain Weighting

Default financial weighting:

Risk & Impact Model — 20%  
Evidence & Audit — 20%  
Incident & Recovery — 15%  
Scenario Testing — 10%  
Observability — 10%  
Policy & Constitution — 10%  
Automation — 10%  
Continuous Assurance — 5%  

Industry variations MAY adjust weights.

---

## 5. Assessment Scoring Model

Each domain scored 1–5  

Weighted maturity score:

Σ(domain_score × domain_weight)

Example bands:

1.0–1.9 → Level 1  
2.0–2.4 → Level 2  
2.5–3.4 → Level 3  
3.5–4.2 → Level 4  
4.3–5.0 → Level 5  

Organisation MAY customise.

---

## 6. Domain ↔ Level Consistency Rule

Maximum downtime ≤ 2 hours  

Maximum data loss ≤ 100 MB  

Maximum operational degradation ≤ 20%
