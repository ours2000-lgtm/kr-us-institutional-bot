📄 CONTROL_LIBRARY_SPEC — Evolution Notes
v1.3 Key Evolution Summary
📊 KEY EVOLUTION POINTS

Lifecycle Metrics

Approval duration, change frequency, and average active duration enable quantitative assessment of governance maturity and operational stability.

Dependency Graph & Interoperability

Explicit control dependency and conflict management improve prioritisation, resolution clarity, and operational predictability.

Risk & Effectiveness Scoring

Controls are evaluated not only by execution success but by their contribution to risk reduction, audit outcomes, and benchmark performance.

Chaos & Simulation Validation

Periodic simulation and chaos testing ensures controls remain operationally resilient rather than purely theoretical.

Evidence Integrity Enhancements

Chain-of-custody tracking and multi-anchor integrity provide strong tamper-evidence and audit traceability.

AI Control Governance

Bias monitoring, drift detection, fairness metrics, and explainability artefacts ensure trustworthiness of AI-driven controls.

Improvement Governance

High-risk control changes now require approval, rollback planning, and post-change reassessment evidence.

Retirement Transparency

Retirement metadata ensures regulatory traceability and historical reconstruction capability.

Cross-Layer Integration

Consistency validation across Capability, TOM, Risk, Impact, Dependency, and Assurance layers enables automatic gap detection.

Transparency Reporting

Control performance and improvement status can be disclosed according to policy-defined stakeholder visibility.
